# Visit brief — Thu 30 Oct 2026

One screen. Do the work; capture only what is listed under each task. Organise nothing on the land.

## Order of the day

Cool morning first for cutting, heavy work next, precision work in good midday light, light work at the end.

1. **Olive suckers → pots** (morning, cool)
   - Prune suckers at the base of the trees you planned. Keep the suckers that come away with some root attached. The ones without root rarely take.
   - Pot in whatever gritty mix is there, water in, shade them.
   - Capture: one photo per pruned tree with its number visible or said aloud. Count of suckers potted. Photo of the pots where they sit.
   - Look at the base of two or three trunks: is there a graft swelling, or does the tree come straight out of the ground? One photo. This decides whether suckers are true to type.

2. **Mulch** (after pruning, while you have the cart out)
   - Ring each freshly cleaned tree 5 to 10 cm deep, out to the drip line, a hand's width clear of the trunk.
   - Capture: which tree numbers got mulched. One photo of the pile at the entrance before and after, to learn how far a pile goes.

3. **Prunings and cut grass → future mulch**
   - Gather to one place: shaded, away from the house, where the cart can reach, near where it will be used. Chop if there is time, otherwise just pile.
   - Capture: one photo of the pile and a line saying where it is.

4. **Entrance wall survey** (midday, flat light, precise)
   - Measure: length, height at both ends, thickness, each pillar width and height, gate opening width, hinge positions.
   - Photos: whole wall straight on from each side; every damaged patch close up with the tape in frame; each pillar top; the gate hinges; one photo of a clean section showing the brick courses so bricks can be counted later.
   - Capture: say the numbers into a voice note as you measure. No typing.

5. **Water tanks and roofs** (midday, while at the house)
   - Look, don't clean yet unless there is time. Measure or estimate each tank's size. Note the downpipe diameter.
   - Photos: each tank inside and out, the gutters, where each downpipe enters.
   - Capture: a voice note on what is in the tanks and what state the gutters are in. The filter shopping list comes from this.

6. **Hay** (afternoon)
   - Fork and cart to one dry spot, ideally under cover. Hay is future mulch or bedding.
   - Capture: a photo of the finished pile and a rough count of stacks moved.

7. **Cactus pads** (quick, end of day)
   - Cut full pads at the joint from the prickly pear. Take 6 to 10 home. They need 1 to 2 weeks in dry shade to callus before potting, so no pots are needed tomorrow. Buy terracotta next week.
   - Capture: photo of each cactus plant where it grows, with something for scale. These become beings on the plot for the first time.

8. **Harvest estimate** (any time you pass through the grove)
   - Count how many trees were picked on 17 Oct and whether they were picked fully.
   - Rate ten unpicked trees light / medium / heavy. Say it into a voice note with the tree numbers.
   - With those two numbers the remaining crop and the number of harvest days fall out. 90 kg to 12 L is a 13 percent yield, which is good oil.

9. **Equipment photos** (last thing, at the house)
   - One photo per item, label or model visible. This builds the equipment list and matches it to the receipts.

## Shopping before or after

- Nothing is needed tomorrow that you do not already have.
- Next week: terracotta pots for the pads, gritty mix if the pots at the land are gone, and the wall materials once the measurements are in.

## Capture rules for the day

- Every task: one before photo, one after photo, one spoken line.
- Say tree numbers aloud in voice notes. Do not type on the land.
- Receipts: photograph at the till or forward the email.
- Phone location on for the camera, so each photo carries its coordinates.
