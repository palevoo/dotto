# The milestone

Set on 6 November 2026. Everything here is provisional except the first line.

dotto's own page at dotto.works is made by dotto, from our notebook. If it serves our own
needs, it can serve others.

The page exists as a draft in `site/index.html`, written by hand on 6 November as a way to
check we were headed the same way. We were. Now it has to become true.

## What "made by dotto" means

In the owner's words. The page shows actual data from the notebook where it applies: pinned
or public lines and photos, the yields, the shelf. Our own lines about the app, and the
author's decision, idea and missing lines, are the source of dotto's changelog. Loops behind
the page, an early sign-up say, keep records of their own. The apps carry the name and the
look of the page.

## Defaults

The most convenient thing by default. Progressively customisable: a custom domain, your own
host, stricter privacy. Interoperable throughout: plain files, the door, standard formats, so
nothing we choose closes a door later. Simplicity first, and it has to scale as far as a
person's needs go.

## What has to exist

Problems, roughly in order. No solutions chosen here.

1. **Public and pinned.** A way for a line or a photo to be meant for the outside. One
   gesture, by the owner, after the fact.
2. **Pages.** A thing an author can write through the door from lines, and publish under an
   address. Where pages are kept, where they are served.
3. **Records.** Data that is not a line: a sign-up, an order, a yield, a plant's state. Where
   it lives, who may write it, how a page reads it. The fact primitive and the tables
   question are this problem.
4. **Loops.** A sentence said once, kept, run, with a receipt each time. Where loops run is
   the parked fork: the Mac, a server of ours, or both by role. The first loop: publish this
   page.
5. **Ways in from outside.** A form on a page, a mail address, an order; each lands as a line
   under the sender's name.
6. **Addresses.** dotto.works for the product, a name under it per person, a custom domain
   later.
7. **Media.** Photos and voice out of the phone to wherever pages are made. The parked media
   fork.
8. **The changelog.** Our margin and bug lines, and the author's own lines, as the source of
   a page.
9. **The apps.** iPhone and Mac redrawn under the name dotto, in the look of the page.
10. **Safety.** What a person's page may contain and run. Each person's pages on their own
    origin. What runs on whose machine, and with whose key. Report and takedown.
11. **The old names.** Ids in code and bundle ids still say margin and weeju. They stay until
    a deliberate rename, so nothing breaks by accident.

## Open

The fork between a Mac that is on and a server of ours. The shape of records. Who authors a
capture that arrives on its own. The order above.
