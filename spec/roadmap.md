# The roadmap

What is not built. The order is not decided. What is built is in `design.md` and `door.md`.

## The milestone

Dotto's own page at dotto.works is made by Dotto, from a notebook. If that serves its own
makers, it can serve others. Today `site/` is written by hand and its lines are invented.

What that takes, as problems, with no solutions chosen:

1. **Public and pinned.** A way for a line or a photo to be meant for the outside: one
   gesture, by the owner, after the fact.
2. **Pages made from the notebook.** Today every page is code. The question is who makes
   a page and out of what: an originator, or the owner in a sentence, out of the shapes
   already there, changed by talking, with no build between the sentence and the page. A
   page has to look like Dotto, show the same on the phone and as a file for an audience,
   and let a tap do something real.
3. **Records.** Data that is not a line: a sign-up, an order.
4. **Ways in from outside.** A form on a page, a mail address; each lands as a line under
   the sender's name.
5. **Addresses.** dotto.works for the product, a name under it per person, a domain of
   their own later.
6. **Media.** Photos and voice out of the phone to wherever pages are served.
7. **Safety.** What a person's page may contain and run. One rule is already chosen: pages
   are files, with no scripts from people, each person on their own origin.

## Not built, by part

**Captures**
- What a link points at when it is behind a login, made by a script, or a video; a video's
  transcript.
- Files other than pictures from the Mac; the share menu on the Mac.
- Mail, orders and calendar events arriving on their own.

**Originators**
- Mail, Calendar and Reminders as originators, in and out.

**Shapes**
- A shape that does something of its own.
- A named place: a place is coordinates today, and nothing calls it home or the garden.
  The day's weather on the day header waits on this.
- "Same as" for two things that are one.

**Loops**
- A loop as a program: a small piece of code the home runs with nothing but what it was
  given.
- Reach: the owner's grant outward, as a grant is inward. Where a program may go and with
  which key, with the key staying in the home.
- Loops that run something other than a Claude session.

**Pages**
- A page as a definition over shapes, which is what the milestone needs.
- Talking to a thing from its own page.
- A reply that carries a photo, a fact or a todo is kept in the file and not yet shown.

**The home**
- It runs only inside the Mac app, on a Mac that is awake. A phone whose notebook has no
  home has nothing that answers.
- A home that runs by itself on a machine that stays on.

**Release**
- The phone app through TestFlight.
- A Mac download: signed for distribution and notarised, which needs the hardened runtime
  switched on, and a way to update.

## Known limits

- Moving a notebook to another folder on the phone stops at an event line it cannot read.
- A line written on the Mac has no place, so it has no weather.
- The weather comes from Open-Meteo, which is free for use that earns nothing. Once Dotto
  is sold it needs their paid plan or their server run by us.
- Nothing in the folder is signed. A file's name says who wrote it, and nothing proves it.
- A session is told what was learned ahead of its first answer. If its first turn sends a
  read and a write together, the read is answered first and the write beside it is taken as
  it came.
- Opening an app reads every event file once, from its start. After that only what is
  added is read. Nothing of the fold is kept on disk between launches.
- An answer through the door that lists or searches still goes over every line.
- The home screen has one app icon. There is no set to choose from yet.
- `MacModel.renameWhatWasKept` moves three names a home kept before 16 November 2026. It
  can be deleted once that home has opened once.
