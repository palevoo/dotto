# Pages, the problem

10 November 2026. What the notebook has asked of pages, and a few ideas noted beside it.
Nothing here is decided.

## The problem

Every page is code. The day page, a line's page, the inbox, Todos, the books, the plants,
a tag's page, search: each one a build, each one ours. The notebook keeps asking for pages
nobody has built, and the asking has a shape:

- Every type of item wants its own page and its own way of being handled. Todos tick off.
  A streak is on or off and shows its progression, with breaks. (9 November, 21:43 and
  22:04.)
- A page can come from a sentence. Collect elements inside dotto: a photo of a pencil, and
  dotto says graphite is collected, and there is a page to browse, explore and learn from.
  (9 November, 22:23, under Project Mendeleev.)
- A page is developed inside the page itself, by chatting: messages or notes with context,
  attached to the entity that aggregates it. (22:23.)
- Custom programmable pages on our data is what the owner wants to tackle next. (22:31.)
- Naming as ceremony: a programmable character that levels up with XP, with items, a hat,
  eyes, engineered by chatting or by dotto, showing across the app. (22:35.)
- Coordinates should flow to places you store, label, identify. (22:03.)
- The milestone, from 6 November: the landing page itself, made by dotto from the lines.
- The public rule, from the study of 6 November: pages are files, no scripts from people.
- A bug that is really a page question: filter by tag, delete a line, land somewhere
  random; a page should scroll gently to its bottom when you are far up. (21:31.)

Underneath these, one question: who makes a page, and out of what. Today only we make
one, out of Swift. The notebook wants the reader, or the owner in a sentence, to make one,
out of the shapes already there, and to change it by talking, with no build between the
sentence and the page.

What a page has to keep doing whoever makes it: look like dotto; show the same on the
phone and as a file for an audience; let a tap do something real, mark done, set a fact,
and have that land in the log under a name; and go with one hold, like everything else.

Most of these are lines under a tag or things of a kind, drawn one of a handful of ways:

something other than pages: a loop that reaches outside, a way in, a named place, Mail.
One, the character, is not a page at all but something that shows on every page.

## Ideas noted, mine, undecided

- A page as a definition over shapes, not a program: what it gathers, a tag's lines, a
  kind's things, one thing; which blocks draw it; what a tap does. Data in the log, so the
  reader writes one from a sentence and rewrites it on request. The ceiling is the set of
  blocks; a new block is a build.
- A page as a thing of kind page, made by an originator, newest definition stands, dropped
  with one hold. Notes under any thing, so a page is developed at its own foot. A page may
  stand for a kind, so one definition draws every plant.
- One definition drawn twice: on the phone from the fold, and to a file by the projector.
  No scripts in either, so the public rule holds without a second system.
- Programs, if ever, as one more block, sandboxed, handed data and nothing else. For the
  character, which wants a look of its own and an animation, and for nothing in the table
  above.
- The trade-off as I see it: a ceiling against consistency, safety and no builds. The table
  above sits under a ceiling of a few blocks. Not weighed with the owner yet.
- A named place as a thing, so coordinates have somewhere to flow; a places page waits on
  it, not on pages.
