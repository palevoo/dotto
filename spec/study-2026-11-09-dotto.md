# Study: dotto's becoming

9 November 2026. How dotto is understood and shown now that it is the system in
spec/system.md and not the Margin app of 2 November. Research and directions by the author;
the owner's framing stands at the top; what was applied is marked.

## The owner's framing

dotto is the operating system, a capture-going-intelligence system, and dotto does the
work. It is both the being and the entity. Everyone should have their own dotto, the OS
and the character at once. The farm stays out of the look beyond necessity.

## Applied on 9 November

- **The palette.** A cool near-white ground (#F6F6F4, dark #111213), neutral black ink
  (#141618, dark #ECEDEA), hairlines barely there, and nothing coloured but the dot.
  Persimmon (#E4572E, dark #FF6A3D) is this notebook's dotto; every other originator keeps
  its colour from its id; indigo, plum and teal are the examples. The same tokens on the
  phone, the Mac and the site. Marks that stand for someone or something new are dots; the
  rounded square stays the shape of things made with the app, photos and buttons.
- **Type.** The system speaks in a grotesk and a person's words stay in a serif, as the app
  already separated them: Geist and Newsreader on the site, SF and New York on the devices,
  their native equivalents.
- **The icon.** Two dots on a sheet, dotto's eyes, the smallest face there is, light, dark
  and tinted, drawn by the icon generator. The Mac window header carries the two dots. The
  rest of the "dotto looks" direction, the four expressions, the eyes in the margin where
  it reads, the evolution it earns, is not applied and not decided.
- **The names.** The apps are called dotto; the door's server name, the reader's door, the
  header, push titles and every sentence the apps say about themselves say dotto; the
  conduct note is spec/conduct.md. Bundle ids, target names, schemes and the keys the apps
  keep their own state under still say margin and notebook, until the registers are checked
  and a migration is written, since a new bundle id is a new app.
- **Vocabulary.** Captures, Originators, Shapes, Links, Loops, Pages. Originators is a
  placeholder the owner chose for now; Hands and Pens were rejected as a direction, Guests
  was not taken up, Views was too technical and became Pages.

## Outside

- **ドット絵.** Dotto-e is pixel art, "dot picture": a picture drawn one dot at a time,
  made beautiful under the limits of early screens.
- **Collisions.** No app or product called dotto was found in the stores; the nearest is
  ToDotto, a Japanese to-do app. The EU and US trademark registers were not searched and
  must be before the bundle ids change.
- **Mr. Game & Watch.** The character did not exist until 2001; it was assembled from the
  unnamed flat figures of the handhelds and kept their constraints, flat, in frames. A
  character from the system, afterwards.
- **dots and Muse.** OpenAI's always-on agents were drawn as bubbly mascots and read as a
  copy of Meta's; the pointed critique was that trust and control are the interface, not
  friendliness. One line on the landing page already puts dotto as the contrast: their agent
  pulls your life onto their computer, ours acts through a door you serve and leaves a
  receipt for every read.

## Directions shown, none chosen

Five pages, in order: a mark on paper in three coats, rejected as one idea; the line and
the dot that picked up, with its mark sheet; the living mark rendered from the notebook by
a rule, a day a row, a line a dot at its hour; the rubricator, the one who comes after and
marks in colour without touching the text, with the pilcrow as its sign; and dotto looks,
two dots as the face, four expressions, eyes in the margin where it reads. The owner liked
the palette of the second and paused the rest. The "no face" rule along the way was the
author's, not the owner's.

## Open, for the owner

- The being: whether dotto has a face at all, and if the two dots are it, what they do.
- Whether a person names their dotto, or it is dotto for everyone.
- Growth: what changes in what it is, earned by shapes forming, never a count.
- The registers, EUIPO and USPTO, before anything is renamed underneath.
- A better word than Originators.
