Privacy

As of 13 November 2026. This page changes when the apps do.

dotto has no account and no server. The people who make it never see your notebook, and have nowhere to keep it if they did.

Where your notebook is

In a folder of plain files that you pick. On your phone, it stays on your phone. In your iCloud Drive, Apple keeps it under your Apple account and Apple's terms, and carries it to your other devices.

What stays on the phone

  • Photos, voice notes and the words made from them. Voice becomes words on the phone, with Apple's models on the device.
  • Where a line was written, if you allow location.
  • Walks from Health, if you switch them on. Each becomes a line in your notebook and goes nowhere else.

What leaves the phone

Nothing, until you switch it on in Setup.

  • Weather. For a line that has a place, that place rounded to about a kilometre and a span of days are sent to Open-Meteo, which answers with the weather. Nothing else is sent. Like any site your phone reaches, Open-Meteo sees the internet address the request came from.
  • Notifications. Apple gives the phone an address for notifications, and the app writes it into your notebook folder, so a home that holds the folder can reach the phone through Apple.

What a home does

A home is dotto on a Mac of yours, if you run one. It reads the same folder.

  • It opens the links you write in your own lines and keeps the page's words beside the line. That is a visit to that site from your Mac.
  • It can send a reply to your phone as a notification. The words of the reply pass through Apple's notification service.
  • It runs loops with Claude Code, under your own Anthropic account. What a loop reads of your notebook is sent to Anthropic, under your agreement with them, not ours. No loop runs until you set one.

What we collect

Nothing. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no code in the apps from anyone but us and Apple.

If you have dotto through TestFlight, Apple shows us crash reports and any feedback you choose to send.
