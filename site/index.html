Say it once. It keeps happening.

A loop is a sentence in your words, said once. It runs from then on and leaves a receipt each time.

No settings screen. No code, unless you want some. Say it under a line or on your page. Change the sentence, the loop changes. Delete it, it is gone.

The nursery

Photograph cuttings when you think of it. Months later each plant has a page: its state now, its family, what it needs, and the story from jar to pot. Point the camera at something wild and it joins a second shelf, the one you are still learning.
